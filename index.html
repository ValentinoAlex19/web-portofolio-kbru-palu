The History of KBRU Palu Branch

Established in 2020, PT Kalibesar Raya Utama (KBRU) Palu Branch represents a strategic expansion to provide professional insurance broking services across Palu City and Central Sulawesi. Located on the 3rd floor of Bank Sinarmas, the branch is dedicated to assisting individual clients, corporations, and financial institutions with tailored insurance solutions and risk mitigation.

Independent Licensed Broker

As an independent insurance broker, KBRU Palu represents the interests of our clients (the insured). We provide comprehensive support throughout the entire lifecycle—from risk management and policy clause structuring to administrative handling and claim advocacy—ensuring fair, objective, and prompt claim settlements.
